# Stakeholder Analysis: Realtime Quiz Platform

## High-Influence & High-Interest Stakeholders
The project manager must prioritize direct communication with **S01 (Head of the IT Department)** and **S02 (Supervising Lecturer / Project Manager)**. 
- **S01 (Sponsor)** holds budget authority and defines final success; maintaining alignment with S01 ensures project authorization and resource approval throughout the 14 weeks.
- **S02 (PM)** directly guides team execution and monitors schedule variance weekly to prevent scope creep.

## Veto Stakeholder
The primary veto stakeholder is **S09 (Data Protection Officer)**. Although S09 has a low day-to-day interest level, S09 possesses high influence and authority over student data compliance. If student data handling on campus or cloud server storage is not explicitly approved prior to Week 14, S09 can block the pilot execution regardless of project completion status.
